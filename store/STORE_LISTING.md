## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
TechNiti Attendance is a simple, fast punch-in/punch-out app for organizations that run their HR on Frappe or ERPNext.

Connect the app to your company's own server — just enter your organization's site address on first launch and log in with your work account. Your data goes directly to your employer's server; there is no middleman service.

HOW IT WORKS
• Punch In / Punch Out with one tap
• A quick selfie verifies it's really you
• GPS confirms you're at an approved work location (geofenced)
• Add an optional note to any punch
• See today's punches at a glance

ATTENDANCE HISTORY
• Monthly calendar view with day-by-day detail
• Attendance status, working hours, and punch records
• Works offline — your recent records are cached on the device

KIOSK MODE
• Turn a shared tablet or phone at the office entrance into a punch station
• Employees punch by entering their employee ID and taking a selfie
• Protected by an admin PIN

PRIVACY FIRST
• Camera and location are used only in the foreground, at the moment you punch
• No background tracking, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs
• Login credentials are stored in encrypted secure storage on your device
• All attendance data is stored on your employer's own server

REQUIREMENTS
• Your organization must run a Frappe/ERPNext site with HR (Employee Checkin) enabled
• The app needs camera permission (selfie verification) and location permission (geofenced attendance) at the moment of punch

This app is intended for workplace use by employees of organizations using Frappe/ERPNext. If you're unsure of your site address or login, contact your HR department.
```
