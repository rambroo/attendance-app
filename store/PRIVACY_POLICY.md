# Privacy Policy — TechNiti Attendance

_Last updated: 6 July 2026_

This privacy policy describes how the **TechNiti Attendance** app ("the app") collects, uses, and protects your information. The app is used by employees of organizations that run their own Frappe/ERPNext HR system.

> **Host this document at a public URL (e.g. GitHub Pages, your company website) and enter that URL in Google Play Console → App content → Privacy policy.**

## Who controls your data

The app connects **directly to your employer's own server** (a Frappe/ERPNext site whose address you or your employer enters on first launch). TechNiti does not operate any central server and never receives your data. Your employer is the data controller; all data collected by the app is stored on your employer's server.

## Data the app collects

| Data | When | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Selfie photo** | Each check-in/out | Attendance verification required by your employer | Uploaded to your employer's server |
| **Precise location (GPS)** | Each check-in/out, while the app is open | Verify you are at an approved work location (geofence) | Uploaded to your employer's server |
| **Work email & name** | Login | Authenticate you against your employer's ERPNext account | Sent to your employer's server |
| **Employee record details** (ID, department, designation) | After login | Display your profile and attendance records | Fetched from your employer's server |
| **Optional punch notes** | When you type them | Context for your check-in/out | Uploaded to your employer's server |

The app does **not** collect data in the background. Location and camera are used only in the foreground, at the moment you punch in or out. The app contains no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking.

## Data stored on your device

- Your login session and (to keep you signed in) your login credentials, stored in **encrypted secure storage** (Android Keystore).
- Cached copies of your own attendance records, so the app works offline.

All of this is deleted when you log out, change site, or uninstall the app. Device backups of app data are disabled.

## Data sharing

The app shares data only with the server your organization configured. No data is sold or shared with any other third party.

## Data retention & deletion

Attendance records, selfies, and locations are retained on your employer's server according to your employer's HR policy. To access, correct, or delete your data, contact your HR administrator. To remove all locally stored data, log out or uninstall the app.

## Children

The app is intended for workplace use by adults (18+). It is not directed at children.
